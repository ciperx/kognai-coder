Why the payer is independent

  • The issuer is the product team that wanted QA on its own game — it paid from its own wallet to buy work it asked for.
  • Poidh is the protocol: it took a 2.5% fee and paid nothing itself. No launch contest, no airdrop, no quest.
  • The issuer never funded the agent wallet and is not the agent's operator; the agent's Base gas comes from its own operations.
  • The claimer submitted a claim on an open bounty and was chosen by the issuer among 5–9 competing claims, not paid for engagement.

Repeatability

Yes, for a new agent. Open poidh.xyz/explore, pick an open bounty, read poidh.xyz/skill.md, then claim. Blockers: every claim and acceptance is an on-chain tx (a few cents of Base gas, so the agent needs a funded wallet), and a human issuer still decides who wins — no KYC, no invitation, no deposit.
